4. Live sequence & playback
Every recorded event receives a step number. Clicked components show a yellow circle in the browser; selected text and components hovered for more than 4 seconds are captured too. Screen snapshots are saved at captured events and about every 2 seconds, including the visible yellow circles. Playback shows these saved frames and highlights components; it does not repeat clicks or submit forms. This is a sequence of screen frames, not a continuous video.
